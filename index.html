Wallflow Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Wallflow is a wallpaper-rotation app for Android. This policy explains what data the app touches and, just as important, what it doesn't.

Data collection

Wallflow does not collect, transmit, or share any data. It has no network access, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising. There are no user accounts. Everything the app does happens entirely on your device.

Photos

When you build an album, you select photos through Android's built-in Photo Picker. Wallflow copies the photos you choose into its own private app storage so it can display them as wallpapers on your schedule. These copies never leave your device, are never uploaded anywhere, and are deleted when you remove the photo or album from the app.

Permissions and why Wallflow uses them

  • SET_WALLPAPER — required to actually change your home/lock screen wallpaper.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — used to fire time-of-day wallpaper changes ("time slots") precisely when scheduled.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE — used only if you opt in to "Unlock rotation" in Settings, to reliably detect screen unlocks and rotate the wallpaper.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — shows the persistent notification required while the opt-in unlock-rotation service is running.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — re-arms your schedules after your device restarts.

Third parties

Wallflow does not use any third-party SDKs that collect data (no analytics, ads, or crash-reporting services).
